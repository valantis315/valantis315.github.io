1. Data Collection

We collect minimal data to provide you with the best experience:

Personal Information:

  • We do not collect any personal identification information (PII) unless voluntarily provided

  • No names, addresses, or phone numbers are collected

Usage Data:

  • App usage statistics (anonymized)

Third-Party Services:

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status and payments
    • Does not access your payment details
    • Only tracks subscription status
    • Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics: Improves app performance and user experience
    • Collects anonymized usage patterns (screens viewed, features used)
    • Tracks app crashes for stability improvements
    • Monitors subscription conversion rates
    • No personal identification or sensitive data
    • Data is aggregated and anonymized
    • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Data collected by Firebase Analytics:

  • Subscription events (purchases, restores)

2. Subscription & Payments

Financial Transactions:

  • QuickPass ITIL 4 does NOT store credit card details

Subscription Management:

  • Managed entirely through your App Store or Google Play account
  • Cancel anytime through your store account settings
